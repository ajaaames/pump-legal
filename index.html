Privacy policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

Who we are

PUMP is developed and published by MULTINET SOLUTIONS (Allen Tumagos, Gabriel Manialong, and Paolo Ramos). This policy explains what information PUMP collects, how it’s used, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact multinetsolution2025@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

PUMP keeps a local, on-device copy of your workout data (routines, sessions, sets, bodyweight entries, personal records, and settings) so the app loads instantly, even on a slow connection. This local copy never leaves your device — it’s a cache, not a backup we have access to.

What we collect

To create and run your account, PUMP collects:

  • Your email address and password, used to sign you in (handled by Firebase Authentication — we never see or store your password ourselves).
  • The workout data you enter: routines, workout days, exercises, sets, reps, weight, and RPE you log during a session.
  • Your bodyweight entries and personal records (PRs), if you choose to log them.
  • Training preferences you set: goal, experience level, available equipment, focus muscle, motivation, and weekly workout days.
  • Optional profile details: a display name, height, weight, and age, if you choose to share them. All are optional and can be left blank.
  • Your weight-unit preference (lb/kg).

What we don’t do

  • We don’t access your camera, microphone, location, or contacts — PUMP never asks for these permissions.
  • We don’t show ads.
  • We don’t use analytics or crash-reporting tools, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don’t sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

How your data is stored

Your account and workout data are stored with Firebase (a Google Cloud service), which provides authentication and database hosting for PUMP. Data is encrypted in transit. Exercise demonstration images are served from jsdelivr, a third-party content delivery network — loading one of these images shares your device’s IP address and the image requested with jsdelivr, the same way any website image would. App updates are delivered through Expo’s update service. Neither jsdelivr nor Expo receives your account or workout data.

Your choices

  • Edit your training preferences, display name, or weight unit any time in Settings.
  • Delete individual routines, routine days, exercises, sessions, or sets from within the app.
  • Delete your entire account from Settings → Edit profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your workout data from our servers and your device, then closes your sign-in. (Bodyweight entries and personal records don’t currently have individual delete buttons — removing them requires a full account deletion.)

Changing your password in-app isn’t available yet — it’s on the way in a future update.

Children

PUMP is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Security

Your account is protected by your password — choose a strong, unique one. Data in transit between your device and Firebase is encrypted. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we rely on Firebase’s security infrastructure to protect your data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date at the top and publish the new version here and in the app.
